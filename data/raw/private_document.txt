Faryal Awan was born in Karachi in 1954. She studied at Calder University and later at Westmere Institute of Technology. She received the Northgate Prize in 2004. Colleagues usually referred to her simply as "Dr. Awan". In 1980, Dr. Awan joined Larkspur College of Science. Faryal Awan's doctoral advisor was Waleed Gilani. He later moved to Thornfield College of Science. Despite a persistent rumour, she never studied at Calder Institute of Physics.
